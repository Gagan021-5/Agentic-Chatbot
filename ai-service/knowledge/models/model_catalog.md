# RentPrompts Model Documentation — Knowledge Base

## Flux Schnell
- Provider: Black Forest Labs
- Type: Image Generation
- Cost: 0.5 coins/run
- Tier: Fast
- Strengths: Ultra-fast generation, good for rapid prototyping
- Prompt Style: Keep concise (40-80 words). Subject + style + lighting keywords.
- Best For: Quick concept art, social media images, thumbnails
- Limitations: Lower detail than Flux 2 Pro. No inpainting support.
- Tags: ultra-fast, budget-friendly

## Flux 2 Pro
- Provider: Black Forest Labs
- Type: Image Generation + Editing
- Cost: 2.5 coins/run
- Tier: Fast
- Strengths: High-quality realistic images, supports editing/inpainting
- Prompt Style: Descriptive visual language. Camera angle, lighting direction, material textures.
- Best For: Product photography, realistic portraits, photo editing
- Limitations: Slower than Schnell. Higher cost.
- Tags: realistic, editing, inpainting

## Imagen 4
