I want an app that creates ATS-friendly resumes for software engineering jobs.

### Extracted Requirements

* App Type: TEXT
* Audience: Students and job seekers
* Goal: ATS optimization
* Industry: Technology

### App Metadata

* App Name: ResumeForge AI
* Description: Transform your experience into ATS-optimized resumes tailored for software engineering roles.
* Category: business
* Tags:

  * resume-builder
  * ats-optimization
  * software-engineering
  * ai-writing
  * job-search
  * career-growth
  * professional-resume

### Why It Works

* Clear user outcome
* Strong action verb
* Specific audience
* Search-friendly keywords

# Naming Formula

Good Names Follow:

[PowerWord] + [Domain]

Examples:

* ResumeForge AI
* ThumbnailForge AI
* InterviewSprint AI
* MatchLens AI
* PodcastVoice AI
* StudyFlow AI
* CareerBoost AI
* SkillCraft AI
* ReelSpark AI

Avoid:

* AI Generator
* Text Creator
* Video Maker
* App Builder
* Generic AI Tool

# Description Formula

Always use:

[Action Verb] + [User Outcome] + [AI Capability]

Examples:

GOOD:

* Transform your experience into ATS-optimized resumes with AI.
* Create high-converting marketing content in seconds.
* Analyze uploaded plant photos and identify crop diseases instantly.

BAD:

* Generates resumes.
* Creates content.
* Makes images.

# Tag Formula

Exactly 7 tags.

Mix:

* 2 broad discovery tags
* 3 niche use-case tags
* 2 outcome/benefit tags

Example:

resume-builder
ats-optimization
software-engineering
job-search
career-growth
professional-resume
ai-writing
